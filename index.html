Suzuki Ikou LLC — clinical software designed and built by a practicing physician.

ENGLISHAbout Suzuki Ikou LLC

Suzuki Ikou LLC is a Japanese software company founded in March 2026 by Yuya Suzuki, a practicing physician. We design, build, and operate software for medical clinics — electronic medical records, reservations, front-desk check-in, billing, and staff management — shaped by daily use in real clinical settings.

Our flagship product, FastKarte, is an electronic medical record system for aesthetic clinics. It has been in production use since September 2026, covering reservation import, check-in, charting, consent forms, payments, and online consultations. We build with Anthropic's Claude throughout our development process, which lets a physician-led team ship and maintain clinical-grade software quickly.
